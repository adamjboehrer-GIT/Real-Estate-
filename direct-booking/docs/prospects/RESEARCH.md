# San Clemente STR prospects — Airbnb research

Pulled 2026-08-17 from a live scan of the Airbnb San Clemente search, entire
homes only. 194 listings scanned (Airbnb caps the result set at about 15 pages),
of which 118 are actually in San Clemente city — the rest are Dana Point,
Capistrano Beach, Oceanside, Mission Viejo and points inland, which Airbnb folds
into the same search.

## How the top three were chosen

Sorted by star rating, then review count, with a **100-review floor**, then
**deduplicated by owner**.

The floor matters. A 5.0 with six reviews is noise, and more to the point a
direct-booking site only pays for itself if the owner already has a repeat and
referral base to capture. Review volume *is* the product thesis.

Deduplicating by owner matters more. Six of the fifteen highest-rated listings
in San Clemente belong to one couple. Ranking listings would have produced three
sites for the same two people.

## 1. Pete & Cathy — The Balcony on Del Mar

- **Listing:** airbnb.com/rooms/43275885 · demo at `/sc-village-balcony`
- **Rating:** 5.00 · 400 reviews · Guest Favorite · Top 5% of homes
- **Host:** Pete & Cathy · Superhost · 10 years hosting
- **Type:** Entire rental unit · 6 guests · 2 bedrooms · 4 beds · 1 bath · 87 amenities
- **Location:** Avenida Del Mar in the village centre. About 5 min walk to Parque
  Del Mar; the free seasonal trolley (Mar–Oct) stops at the building; Amtrak and
  Metrolink at the pier.
- **Airbnb quotes (2 guests, all-in):**
  - Sep 14–18 2026 — **$1,867** (4 nights)
  - Dec 7–11 2026 — **$1,384** list, discounted to $1,261 by a temporary promo
- **Fees from the listing:** up to 3 pets with pre-approval at $20/night, dogs
  under 60 lbs; max occupancy 6 (5 adults) with a +$25 extra-guest charge.
- **Kit:** private balcony with fire pit and gas BBQ, electric fireplace, central
  AC, in-unit washer/dryer, softened water, dedicated driveway space, desk.
  Bikes, boogie boards, wetsuits, beach chairs, umbrellas and a wagon.
- **No ocean view** on this unit. The listing itself notes a sister unit "D" has
  a peek-a-boo view, which is how the portfolio first surfaced.

**Why they are first.** A perfect 5.0 across 400 reviews is genuinely rare, and
ten years of hosting means a decade of accumulated repeat guests who currently
pay Airbnb 14% to rebook a place they already know. Two named human hosts, not a
management company.

**They are the biggest prize on the list, not just the best-rated one.** Six
listings, roughly 2,300 combined reviews, all at 4.98 or above. One yes is six
subscriptions.

**Caveat worth raising early.** Their photos are served from Airbnb's
`prohost-api` path, which means the listing is pushed through professional host
software — Hostaway, Guesty, OwnerRez or similar. They already pay for tooling.
That cuts both ways: they understand the category, and their PMS may already
bundle a direct-booking page. Ask in the first five minutes.

  season to anchor against.
- **Fees from the listing:** dogs $30/day per pet, paid on site. No cats. Laundry
  available as a paid extra.
- **Kit:** award-winning courtyard garden with fire pit and hammock, private
  upstairs balcony, vaulted ceiling, French doors, full bath with tub, driveway
  parking. Cottage kitchen — fridge, sink, large toaster oven, coffee maker, no
  range.

**Be honest with yourself about this one.** It is the weakest of the three
economically: the nightly rate is a third of the others, so 14% of it is a
smaller number, and the break-even is 23 direct bookings a year rather than 10.
At roughly 38 reviews a year she is plausibly doing 55–75 bookings, so 23 is
reachable but it is not the easy yes the other two are.

Her photography is also older and lower resolution than the other two
prospects'. That is a genuine, free piece of advice to lead with, and it is the
kind of observation that opens a conversation better than a pitch does.

## Notes for whoever runs this again

**Airbnb no longer itemizes.** The listing page now shows a single all-in figure
("4 nights x $466.75 · $1,867.00") and the cleaning fee, service fee and tax
split only appear at checkout. Every Airbnb number recorded here is therefore
all-in, which is the right number for the head-to-head anyway — it is what the
guest actually pays. The component split in each demo's rate rows is *solved
backwards* out of that total through Airbnb's own arithmetic, not observed:

    all-in = (rent + cleaning) x 1.14 guest service fee x 1.10 San Clemente TOT

The cleaning fee is the free variable in that equation and is an estimate in all
three seeds. Getting the owner's real cleaning fee is the single cheapest way to
make every number on their page exact.

**Availability is readable without the host's iCal URL.** The public listing page
exposes `data-is-day-blocked` on each calendar cell, about four months out.
Clear any date selection first: Airbnb also greys out dates that merely violate
the minimum stay for a chosen arrival, which reads identically to booked. All
three demos are seeded with real blocked dates read this way, and all three go
stale within weeks.

**The search scan is reproducible.** Filter to entire homes, then page through
collecting `[data-testid="card-container"]` innerText, which carries the rating
and review count in an accessible label. Persist to `sessionStorage` between
pages; Airbnb's pagination is a client-side navigation that preserves `window`
but the run is long enough to be worth surviving a reload.
