only. 194 listings scanned (Airbnb caps the result set at ~15 pages), of which 118
are actually in San Clemente city — the rest are Dana Point, Capistrano Beach,
Oceanside, Mission Viejo and further inland, which Airbnb folds into the same
search.

## Ranking criterion

Sorted by star rating, then review count, with a **100-review floor**. The floor
matters: a 5.0 with 6 reviews is noise, and more to the point a direct-booking
site only pays for itself if the owner already has a repeat and referral base to
capture. Review volume *is* the product thesis.

## 1. Balcony Fire Pit Walk to Village, Beach and Pier

- **Listing:** airbnb.com/rooms/43275885
- **Rating:** 5.00 · 400 reviews · Guest Favorite · Top 5% of homes
- **Host:** Pete & Cathy · Superhost · 10 years hosting
- **Type:** Entire rental unit · 6 guests · 2 bedrooms · 4 beds · 1 bath
- **Location:** Avenida Del Mar, San Clemente village center. ~5 min walk to
  Parque Del Mar; free seasonal trolley (Mar–Oct) stops at the building; Amtrak
  and Metrolink at the pier.
- **Airbnb quote:** **$1,867 all-in for 4 nights** (Sep 14–18 2026, 2 guests) =
  $466.75/night all-in.
- **Fees from the listing:** pets up to 3 with pre-approval at $20/night, dogs
  under 60 lbs; max occupancy 6 (5 adults), extra-guest surcharge noted.
- **Amenities:** 87 total. Private balcony, fire pit, natural gas BBQ, electric
  fireplace, central AC, in-unit washer/dryer, softened water, dedicated
  driveway space, fast wifi, dedicated workspace, Keurig station, smart TVs.
  Free bikes, boogie boards, wetsuits, beach chairs, umbrellas.
- **No ocean view** on this unit (the listing notes a sister unit "D" has a
  peek-a-boo view — so they run more than one unit in the building).

**Sales read:** the strongest prospect on the list. A perfect 5.0 across 400
reviews is rare, and 10 years of hosting means a decade of accumulated repeat
guests who are currently paying Airbnb's 14% to rebook a place they already
know. Two named human hosts, not a management company.

**Caveat:** their photos are served from Airbnb's `prohost-api` path, which means
they are pushing the listing through professional host software (Hostaway,
Guesty, OwnerRez or similar). They already pay for tooling, which cuts both ways
— they understand the category, but their PMS may bundle a direct-booking page.
Worth asking early in the conversation.

## Notes on the pricing display

Airbnb's PDP no longer itemizes. It now shows a single all-in figure
("4 nights x $466.75 · $1,867.00") and the cleaning/service-fee/tax split only
appears at checkout. Every Airbnb total recorded here is therefore all-in, which
is the right number for the head-to-head anyway — it is what the guest actually
pays. The component split used in each demo site's rate rows is derived from that
total, not observed, and is labeled as such.
