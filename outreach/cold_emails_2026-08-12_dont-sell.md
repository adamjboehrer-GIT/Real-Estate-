## Signature + opt-out (every send)

Name, DRE #, brokerage, and office address are all carried by the Outlook footer image.

The opt-out line goes in as **live text, as the last line before the footer image.** Not inside the image. Outlook and Gmail block remote images by default, and an opt-out nobody can read is an opt-out that doesn't count.

```
Don't want to hear from me again? Reply "stop" and I'm gone for good.
```

Keep the wording as-is in both versions. It reads like the rest of the email instead of like a legal footer, which is the point.

## Version A · never emailed

**Subject:** you shouldn't sell your house

> Hi {{name}},
>
> That's an odd thing to get from a Realtor. Stay with me.
>
> I've sent the other kind of email. Record prices, free valuation, now is the time. Everyone has.
>
> Here's what I've actually learned. The market almost never decides when someone sells. Life does. A job moves. A parent needs help. A kid moves out and the house goes quiet, or one arrives and it gets small.
>
> If none of that is happening, you probably shouldn't sell.
>
> So consider this an introduction. I'm the Realtor who told you not to sell.
>
> If and when the time does come, I'll be an honest voice the whole way through. I'll tell you what your home is actually worth, what it will take to get there, and then I'll outwork anyone else in town to get it the representation, and the price, it deserves.
>
> That's the whole email. I'm right here when you want me.
>
> Best,

## What changed from the mailer, and why

| Mailer line | Email | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| "odd thing to find in your mailbox" | "odd thing to get from a Realtor" | mailbox reference doesn't hold in an inbox |
| "I've sent the other kind of flyer" | "the other kind of email" | same |
| "consider this an introduction" | kept verbatim | it's the hinge of the piece |
| "I'm the Realtor who told you not to sell" | kept, set on its own line | the line people will remember, needs air around it |
| "an honest voice throughout the process" | "an honest voice the whole way through" + what that means concretely | print can be abstract, email has to earn the next scroll |
| QR code to newsletter | optional P.S. link to adamboehrer.com | no QR in email |

Print-only elements dropped: the blueprint/pool hero images, the QR block, and the full PSIR legal footer. The signature block carries the DRE number, designation, and brokerage, which is what email needs. No AI-altered imagery in this piece, so no AB 723 disclosure applies.
