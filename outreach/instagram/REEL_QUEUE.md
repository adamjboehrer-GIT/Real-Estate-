Film-ready scripts. Read off your phone, press record.

Target: **3–4 Reels a week**, batch-filmed. One afternoon of shooting is about
two weeks of content.

## Nothing scripted yet

The engines behind the scripts are live and tested:

- `scripts/ig_price_band.py` — what $X actually buys, from closed sales
- `scripts/ig_deal_of_week.py` — this week's best value and its catch

Current San Clemente data (423 closed standard sales, last 12 months) already has
the counterintuitive finding that makes a strong first Reel: **price per foot
drops from $849 at the $1.5M band to $787 at $2M, then jumps to $944 at $3M.**
The $2M band is the value pocket, and nobody in the market is saying it.
