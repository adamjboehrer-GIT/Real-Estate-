## 01 · price_band · The $2M value pocket

**slug:** `2026-08-13-price-band-value-pocket-sc` · **status:** SCRIPTED · **est:** 35s
**data:** `ig_price_band.py`, 270 closed standard sales in San Clemente, last 12
months, bands at $1.5M / $2M / $3M within ±15%

**Premise:** The $2M band is where a dollar buys the most house in San Clemente.
Below it you pay the small-home premium. Above it you pay for view.

### HOOK (0:00–0:02)

> Two million dollars in San Clemente buys you a better deal per square foot
> than one and a half million does.

### BEATS

| # | Say | On screen |
|---|-----|-----------|
| 1 | At one and a half million in San Clemente, you're paying about $849 a foot for around 1,700 square feet. | `$1.5M → 1,737 sq ft → $849/ft` |
| 2 | At two million, you're paying $787 a foot. For 2,458 square feet. | `$2M → 2,458 sq ft → $787/ft` |
| 3 | You spend 33 percent more money and get 42 percent more house. | `+33% money. +42% house.` |
| 4 | Then it flips. At three million you're back up to $944 a foot. | `$3M → $944/ft` |
| 5 | Because past about two and a half, you stop buying square footage and start buying view. | `Past $2.3M you're buying view` |
| 6 | That's 270 closed sales in the last year, not a hunch. | `270 closed sales, last 12 months` |
| 7 | I run these numbers every week. | `Every week` |

### SHOT LIST

- **Hook + beats 1–3:** talking head, San Clemente pier or the bluff trail,
  golden hour. Chest up, ocean soft behind you.
- **Beats 4–5:** same setup, or cut to a second angle so the flip lands as a
  visual change.
- **B-roll while you're there:** pier pilings, a residential street with mature
  trees, rooflines against the water.
- **Do not** film on your own street or include anything address-identifying.
- Shoot 9:16. Hold 2 seconds of silence at the top and tail for the edit.

### CAPTION

> Two million dollars buys a better deal per square foot in San Clemente than one
> and a half million does.
>
> Small homes carry a higher price per foot. That's mechanical. But the flip at
> three million isn't. That's the point where you stop paying for square footage
> and start paying for view.
>
