## Compliance notes

**No BRBC needed yet.** The written buyer agreement requirement triggers before *touring* a home.
Setting up a search and sending listings does not trip it. If this turns into a showing, get the
agreement signed first. Correct call to leave it out of a soft email like this.

**CRMLS is the real one here**, since the whole pitch is a curated search. Set it up as an MLS
client portal / saved search so listing data is delivered with proper attribution and IDX
disclaimers. Do not hand-build a list and paste listing content into emails.

**Fair housing, and this one matters given the wide search area.** A buyer with no fixed geography
is exactly where steering happens. Build the search on their stated criteria only: price, beds,
property type, the towns *they* name. Never suggest or omit a town based on who lives there, and
don't characterize neighborhoods by their residents. If they ask "where would we fit in," redirect
to objective factors like price points, housing stock, commute, and beach access.

**No investment promises.** Copy says "make a smart buy," not that the property will appreciate.
Keep it that way. Projected returns are a claim you don't want to own in writing.
