## Compliance notes

**Fair housing, and read this one before you reply to them.** The multigenerational content is safe
here because it describes *property features* they asked for: casita, ADU, downstairs suite with
private entrance, dual primaries. That is the correct framing and the only correct framing.

Do not write, in this email or any reply, that a home is "great for aging parents," "perfect for
your mom," "ideal for families," or anything that ties a property to who would live in it. Describe
the house. Never the people. This same content cannot be reused in any public marketing piece
without stripping the personal context, since advertising is held to a stricter standard than 1:1
correspondence with a buyer about their own stated criteria.

Also do not steer them toward or away from any town or neighborhood based on who lives there. Use
price, housing stock, lot size, ADU potential, commute, and beach access.

**No BRBC needed yet.** The written buyer agreement requirement triggers before touring a home.
Intake questions and a search do not trip it. Get it signed before the first showing.

**CRMLS** — Run the search as an MLS client portal / saved search so listing data carries proper
attribution and IDX disclaimers. Don't paste MLS content into email bodies.

**ADU claims** — Fine to say a property has a casita or a permitted ADU if the listing says so.
Do not tell them an unpermitted space can be legalized, or that they can add an ADU to a given lot.
That's a city planning question, and it depends on the jurisdiction and the lot. Point them to the
city, or to a design-build contractor.
