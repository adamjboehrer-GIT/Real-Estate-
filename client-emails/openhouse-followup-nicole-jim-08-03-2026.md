## Compliance notes

**BRBC still has to be signed before the first showing, not after.** You're handling it in person,
which is fine, just make sure it happens before you put them in the car. NAR practice changes
(8/17/2024) require a written agreement before touring a home, and California AB 2992 (1/1/2025)
requires a written buyer-broker representation agreement. It must conspicuously disclose your
compensation or how it's determined. Signing it at the first meeting on the 12th works.

**CRMLS** — Send the home list as a client portal link rather than pasting listing data into an
email. Pasted MLS content triggers attribution and IDX disclaimer requirements.

**Fair housing** — Build the tour list off their stated criteria only: price, bedrooms, pool,
location. No neighborhood characterizations based on who lives there.
