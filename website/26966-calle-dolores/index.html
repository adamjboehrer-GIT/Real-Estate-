The Home

Welcome to the heart of Capistrano Beach's coveted Golden Triangle, where timeless Tuscan-modern design meets today's most desirable upgrades. Tucked away on a quiet street and set back from the road for exceptional privacy, this extensively remodeled residence has been thoughtfully reimagined from top to bottom, offering a truly turnkey opportunity. No expense was spared during the comprehensive renovation, including a brand-new repipe, complete electrical rewire, updated mechanical systems, and luxurious finishes throughout.

Step inside to discover an open-concept floor plan enhanced by white oak-inspired luxury vinyl plank flooring. Designed for both everyday living and effortless entertaining, the gourmet kitchen showcases premium soft-close cabinetry, an elegant wood-accent island illuminated by designer pendant lighting, and an ideal open concept layout. A dramatic panoramic double-slide glass door floods the interior with natural light while creating a seamless indoor-outdoor connection to the beautifully landscaped backyard. Every bathroom has been meticulously remodeled with carefully selected high-end materials, delivering a spa-inspired experience.

Outside, professionally designed hardscape transforms both the front and rear yards into expansive, highly usable outdoor living spaces. The oversized driveway accommodates more than four vehicles and offers potential RV parking, all without the restrictions of an HOA. The oversized garage includes durable epoxy flooring and abundant space for vehicles, workshops, or fitness equipment. Adding long-term value, the home includes a paid-off, owned solar system paired with a whole-home backup battery (Franklin Battery), providing energy efficiency, lower utility costs, and peace of mind during power outages.

Perfectly positioned just moments from Capistrano Beach, Dana Point Harbor, world-class beaches, restaurants, shopping, coastal trails, and easy freeway access, this property delivers an exceptional blend of luxury, functionality, and one of South Orange County's most sought-after coastal lifestyles.
