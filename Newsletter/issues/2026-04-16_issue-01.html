What I've Been Watching for the Last Six to Eight Months

Homes priced on the high side of their value range have really struggled. Buyers are pickier this cycle. They've been willing to sit on the sidelines and wait for price cuts, and they've been rewarded for waiting. At the same time, homes priced aggressively and correctly from day one are moving fast. San Clemente closed March with a median of 11 days on market, while Orange County overall is still at 38. The difference isn't demand. It's pricing discipline. If you're thinking about a move, in either direction, getting the strategy right from day one matters more this year than it has in a while.

What's Happening in the Neighborhood

Most Hillcrest Activity Is on Avenida Salvador

Most of what's moving in Hillcrest right now is concentrated on one street. Several new builds are under construction. A couple of parcels have been scraped for ground-up rebuilds. Multiple heavy remodels are advancing. If you want a real-time read on where Hillcrest is heading, Avenida Salvador is the street to watch.

Owners Are Reinvesting, Not Selling

Across the rest of Hillcrest, the pattern is full-scope renovations and ground-up rebuilds. Not cosmetic refreshes. That's what owners do when they're planning to stay for the long run. It tightens future inventory and strengthens comps across the neighborhood.

What's New in San Clemente

Jimmy B's Opens May 1 on El Camino Real

Jim Bellino's steakhouse concept, already a hit in Palm Springs, is coming to 204 S El Camino Real this May. Jimmy B's Grill, Cocktails & Steakhouse brings live music and a proper bar program to the Village. Worth putting on the calendar.
