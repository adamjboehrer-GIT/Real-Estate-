What's Happening in the Neighborhood

This Issue  |  San Clemente

Let's Talk Price

San Clemente is the southernmost beach town in Orange County, the last walkable village before the San Diego County line, and this summer the whole ballgame here comes down to one word: price. Pricing a home right has always mattered. What has changed is how little patience buyers have for a number that reaches. Between interest rates, the headlines about jobs and the wider world, and a general sense of uncertainty, buyers are moving carefully, and they are quick to walk away from anything that feels overpriced. You can see it in the fact that more than a third of the homes on the market have already cut their price at least once. But here is the part that surprises people. The homes that are priced right are still selling at essentially full value. The median San Clemente home sold this spring at one hundred percent of its asking price, and across the city sales are closing at roughly ninety-eight percent of list. The demand has not gone anywhere. It has just gotten disciplined. Price a home to today's market and it sells, close to asking. Price it to last year's peak and it sits, then usually chases the market down anyway. That gap is the whole story right now.

What's New in San Clemente

Another Coffee Shop? Not Quite

Just what San Clemente needed, another coffee shop. Except the Petrol Club, newly open on North El Camino Real, is not really that. It is a seriously good third-wave coffee bar wrapped around a rotating display of collectible cars, built by two car-obsessed South County locals. On any given morning you might be sipping a cortado next to a Ford GT, a Porsche 996, or whatever rolled in that week, no ropes and no museum feel. It fills a genuinely fun niche, a home base for the enthusiast crowd and the local Cars and Coffee regulars who meet up at the outlets. There is even a sandbox and a play space for the kids out back, so the whole family has a reason to linger. Exactly the kind of spot that makes a town feel like home.
